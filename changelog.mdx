The LC circuit breathes to life: capacitor charging, Kirchhoff discharge derivation, second-order ODE, stacked vector oscillation curves in TikZ, and total electromagnetic energy conservation. 🌸

### Added

- **Chapter 3 · Lesson 3 continued (LC circuit & electromagnetic oscillations):**
  - **សៀគ្វី LC ពេលផ្ទុកថាមពល (៥.១):** SPDT switch connection to terminal `a` shown in vector CircuitikZ (រូបទី៣៩), charging capacitor to Q₀ = CΕ (២៣), electrostatic energy storage Eₑ = ½CΕ² (២៤), and initial conditions box for t = 0
  - **សៀគ្វី LC ពេលផ្ទេរថាមពល (៥.២):** SPDT switch connection to terminal `b` isolating the closed LC loop (រូបទី៤០), Kirchhoff loop derivation −v_C + v_L = 0 with discharging condition i = −dq/dt, second-order ODE d²q/dt² + (1/LC)q = 0 (២៥), harmonic oscillator analogy, general charge solution q(t) = q_max cos(ωt + φ) (២៦), and instantaneous current expression i(t) = −ωq_max sin(ωt + φ) = −I_max sin(ωt + φ) (២៧)
  - **Stacked Vector Oscillation Graph (រូបទី៤១):** custom dual-subplot TikZ figure plotting charge q(t) in `brownheader` and current i(t) in `primary`, with quarter-period vertical alignment dashed lines (T/2, T, 3T/2, 2T) illustrating phase quadrature
  - **Natural Period / Time Constant:** boxed τ_LC = T = 2π√(LC) (២៨) with parameter glossary
  - **ថាមពលនៃសៀគ្វី LC:** total electromagnetic energy formula E = E_L + E_C = ½Li² + ½Cv² (២៩), dedicated `\formulabox` for quantities and SI units, and `\defbox` on total energy conservation in an ideal LC oscillator
